What I found

01

The first screen introduces the shop before the work

The homepage opens on a slideshow led by Joey Hamilton's portrait and the Revolt artwork, after a black screen, with no button. Fix: open on finished work and work in progress from your artists, with one button under it: Book a consultation.

02

Your own videos never reach your own site

Your artists post work on Instagram: a spider piece from stencil to finished ink, an eagle chest piece, sleeves. None of it plays on revolttattoos.com. The only video on the homepage is a Vegas Golden Knights episode from another channel. Fix: put them where the slideshow sits, playing on their own.

03

Your Instagram section shows raw code instead of photos

Where your work should show, the page prints [instagram-feed feed=1] as text. The feed never loads, so visitors see code instead of photos. Fix: repair the feed, or remove it until it renders.

04

One of your four booking buttons opens no form

Three of the four booking buttons land on a form; the fourth, BAYBROOK, changes the address to #baybrook and stops: no form for that shop exists. The other forms sit further down the page, so visitors can book, just not at the shop picked. Fix: publish that form, or take the button down until it exists.

05

Booking starts with a location pick, then a long form

Booking takes a click to CONSULTATIONS and a location choice, then a form with 8 required items: name, email, phone, artist, idea description, size and placement, dates, age certification, before a visitor can ask a question. Fix: one Book a consultation button and flagship number 702.912.4855 in the first screen.

06

Buyers who search for what you sell land on your competitors

The sitemap lists locations, artists and booking forms, and no page for custom tattoo work. In searches typed before a buyer knows a shop's name, Revolt did not appear. Fix: one page per service, starting with custom tattoos.

Do it yourself

The fixes, in the order to do them

1. Put booking on the first screen

Surface: Homepage slideshow.

Do this: Add this button under the slideshow: Book a consultation Link it to the booking page and put 702.912.4855 beside it. (Finding 5.)

Test it: Open the homepage in a private window and check that the button and the number show without scrolling.

2. Give BAYBROOK its form

Surface: Booking page.

Do this: Publish the Baybrook form on the page, or remove the button until it exists. (Finding 4.)

Test it: Click BAYBROOK and check that a booking form for that shop appears.

3. Repair the Instagram section

Surface: Homepage, above the footer.

Do this: Reconnect the feed to @revolttattoos, or use 6 stills from the shop's posts.

Test it: Scroll to the footer and check that photos show, not code.

4. Open on the work

Surface: Homepage slideshow.

Do this: Lead with finished work and work in progress from your artists; the founder portrait moves later.

Test it: Load the homepage in a private window and check that the first screen shows tattoo work.

5. Give custom tattoos their own page

Surface: New page.

Do this: Open with the quick answer, dated: Custom tattoos at Revolt Tattoos, Fashion Show Mall, 3200 S Las Vegas Blvd STE 2680, Las Vegas. By appointment: (702) 912-4855. Updated November 2026.

Test it: Open the new page and check that the first screen says what, where and how to book, with the date.

6. Complete the business data

Surface: The hidden business details in your homepage code (what Google reads for your name, address and hours).

Do this: Add to those details: your name, phone +1-702-912-4855, the Fashion Show Mall street address, your opening hours, and that you are a tattoo shop.

Test it: Paste your homepage address into Google's Rich Results Test (search.google.com/test/rich-results) and check that your business shows with no errors.
